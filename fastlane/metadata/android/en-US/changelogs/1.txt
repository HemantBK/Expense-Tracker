Expense Tracker 1.0.0 — first stable release.

Highlights:
- Auto-parse transaction SMS from Indian banks and UPI apps
- On-device ML merchant categorization (no network, no Google services)
- Budget tracking with monthly burn-rate alerts and anomaly insights
- Receipt scanning via on-device Tesseract OCR
- Passphrase-encrypted CSV exports
- 100% free and open source, GPL-3.0
