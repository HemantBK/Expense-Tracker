Expense Tracker is a local-first, privacy-preserving expense tracker for Android. It auto-parses your bank and UPI transaction SMS, categorizes them with on-device AI, and stores everything in an encrypted local database.

No cloud. No account. No analytics. No proprietary SDKs.

FEATURES
- Automatic transaction tracking by parsing SMS from bank and UPI senders.
- On-device ML categorization (no data ever leaves your phone).
- AES-256 encrypted local database via SQLCipher.
- Biometric app lock with auto-lock.
- Monthly spending dashboard with charts and budget alerts.
- Receipt OCR via Tesseract (offline).
- Encrypted CSV export and backup with passphrase you control.

PRIVACY
- 100% free and open source (GPL-3.0-or-later).
- No INTERNET permission required.
- No third-party SDKs, no analytics, no tracking.
- Reads SMS only to extract transaction details; never accesses banking apps.
- Source code auditable at https://github.com/HemantBK/Expense-Tracker

PERMISSIONS
- READ_SMS, RECEIVE_SMS: detect transaction SMS from bank/UPI senders.
- USE_BIOMETRIC: biometric app lock.
- CAMERA (optional): receipt OCR.

We do NOT request: contacts, location, accessibility services, or network access.
